GUIDELINES

Rules

General guidelines for fair, safe matches. These may be updated over time.

How a match works

Each player is trying to avoid spelling DRILL. The player with the lead sets a move and gets a limited number of tries to land it. If they land it, their opponent gets a limited number of tries to match it. Miss every try and you take the next letter. The leader keeps the lead until they run out of tries to set, then the lead passes. First to spell DRILL loses. The room host chooses the number of tries.

Game modes

  • DRILL: a catch is a catch. If you land the move, it counts.
  • SHARP: the move must be clean. It has to be performed start to finish with control: no fumbles, drops, regrips, or recoveries.

DRILL and SHARP each have their own rating and leaderboard.

Making the call

The setter makes the call on every attempt, both their own and their opponent's. Call it honestly and consistently. The setter's call stands during the match. If you think a call was wrong, press Challenge this call. It is logged for review but does not change the result. Repeated bad calls can lead to moderation.

Leaving and disconnects

  • Closing an empty room is free.
  • Leaving a match once it has started counts as a loss for you and a win for your opponent.
  • If you close the site or lose connection, your opponent sees a 60 second timer. If you are not back by then, you forfeit.
  • If you come back in time, the match continues where it left off.

Rating and ranks

A win is +50 ELO and a loss is −50 ELO in that mode. Your ELO never goes below 0.

Safety and conduct

  • Only attempt moves you have trained for, with enough space and supervision.
  • Use demilitarized or training equipment only. Never use a live firearm.
  • Be respectful. Harassment, cheating, or abusing the call can get you removed.
  • Guests can try the demo rooms, but guest ranks are not saved and guests cannot play real opponents. Log in for ranked play.
